# Glossary

**Client**: An organisation or person Biolinks sells to (e.g. a hospital, clinic or pharmacy), kept on record and managed by Staff. A Client does not need a login. POS sales are linked to a Client.
_Avoid_: customer (when you mean the buyer on record)

**Customer**: A login role for a member of the public. Unused at launch: customer sign-up is hidden.
_Avoid_: client

**Staff**: A login role for Biolinks employees who run the back-office: POS, orders, products, clients, finances.

**Admin**: A Staff member who can also create other Staff accounts. The first Admin is created from the command line, never by signing up.

**POS sale**: An Order recorded by Staff at the point of sale for goods paid offline. Line prices come from the product's current price, never from what the till submits.

**Restock request**: A public visitor's request to be told when an out-of-stock product is available again.
_Avoid_: stock interest (the code name for it)
